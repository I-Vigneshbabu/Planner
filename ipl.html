What is IPL?

IPL stands for Input Planner List. It is a comprehensive productivity application designed to help you input your tasks, plan your week, and list your achievements.

The core of the Input Planner List is the dashboard. It gives you an immediate overview of your daily tasks, priority levels, and current streak.

Consistency is key. The IPL habit tracker lets you define daily routines and tracks your completion rates over time.

Stay focused with the integrated timer. IPL combines the Pomodoro technique directly with your task list for maximum efficiency.
